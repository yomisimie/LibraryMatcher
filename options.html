Library Matcher setup

Sign in to the Steam store in this Firefox, then click Save & Sync. No API key or password needed: your library and wishlist are read the same way the Steam store reads them to show "In Library" on its pages. Nothing leaves your browser except requests to Steam.

Advanced: use a Steam Web API key instead

Only needed if you'd rather not stay signed in to Steam in Firefox. The key stays in this browser and is only sent to api.steampowered.com. You can revoke it at any time on the same page you get it from.

Filled in automatically when you're signed in. Otherwise find it on your Steam account page ("Steam ID" under your account name). If your profile link looks like steamcommunity.com/profiles/7656…, that number is it.

Get one at steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey. For "Domain Name" enter anything, e.g. localhost. Steam only issues keys to accounts that have spent at least $5, and your profile's game details must be public.

Price comparison

Which Steam regional store to price against. Leave empty to pick it from the currency GOG shows you (e.g. lei → Romania). Prices in a different currency are converted at the daily ECB rate.

Some GOG pages, like your wishlist, show prices without a currency sign. Steam prices there are converted to this currency. Auto uses the last currency the extension saw on a GOG store page.

Cached data

Steam prices are kept for 12 hours, GOG search results on the Steam wishlist page for 6 hours, and exchange rates for a day. Release dates and developers, used to tell apart games with the same name, are kept for good. Clear them if a game is matched wrongly after a store fixed its details. Your synced Steam library, wishlist and ignored games are not affected.

Ignored games

To ignore a game on GOG, right-click it and choose Ignore, or hover it and click ✕. Changes here apply immediately. The list syncs across your devices through Firefox Sync when "Add-ons" is enabled in your Sync settings.
