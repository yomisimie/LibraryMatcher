Library Matcher setup

Enter your Steam ID and a Steam Web API key to sync your Steam library and wishlist. Both stay in your browser and are only sent to Steam.

Find it on your Steam account page ("Steam ID" under your account name, sign-in needed). If your profile link looks like steamcommunity.com/profiles/7656…, that number is it.

Get one at steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey. For "Domain Name" enter anything, e.g. localhost. Steam only issues keys to accounts that have spent at least $5.

Price comparison

Which Steam regional store to price against. Leave empty to pick it from the currency GOG shows you (e.g. lei → Romania). Prices in a different currency are converted at the daily ECB rate.

Ignored games

To ignore a game on GOG, right-click it and choose Ignore, or hover it and click ✕. Changes here apply immediately. The list syncs across your devices through Firefox Sync when "Add-ons" is enabled in your Sync settings.
